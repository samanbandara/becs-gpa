BECS GPA Calculator

BSc Honours in Electronics and Computer Science · Faculty of Science, University of Kelaniya (Handbook 2023/2024)

Grade points follow handbook section 2.2 and GPA is the credit-weighted mean (2.4). Credits are the last digit of each course code. ACLT and CMSK units are not counted for GPA. All Year 1 and 2 modules are compulsory and must be graded. In Years 3 and 4 only the modules you register for count. Class eligibility is a guide only: the 30-credits-per-level rule and time limits are not checked. Confirm with the Faculty.
